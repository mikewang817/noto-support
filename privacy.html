PRIVACY POLICY

Your paper.
Your business.

Effective October 31, 2026

Noto does not send your handwriting or voice notes to us. There are no in-app accounts, ads, analytics SDKs, or tracking.

Your papers

Your papers and guide settings are saved locally in the app on your device. Device backups may include them. The canvas trash clears the current paper; Undo may restore it during the session. To delete a saved paper, select it in the paper overview and tap the trash.

Clippings and sharing

Circling ink places an image on the system clipboard and in a temporary shelf. You choose whether to paste or drag it into another app. When you share content, the receiving app’s privacy policy applies. Noto does not send your notes to an AI service.

Microphone and voice notes

Noto uses the microphone only after you tap a selected image’s record button and grant permission. Recording stops when you tap Stop, leave the selection, encounter an audio interruption, reach two minutes, or enter the background.

Audio stays temporarily on your device and is copied with the image as a video when recording ends. You decide whether to share it. We do not receive or transcribe audio. Clearing the shelf or closing the app removes session notes; Undo may retain them during the session. You can revoke microphone permission in device Settings.

Purchases

The free clipping count is stored on your device. Apple processes purchases and provides verified purchase records to the app for annual and lifetime access, including eligible family-shared purchases. We do not receive your payment details or send your handwriting with purchases.

This website and support

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not add analytics, advertising, cookies or embedded third-party media. GitHub may process technical information about visits under its privacy statement.

If you contact us through GitHub Issues, your message and GitHub profile are visible publicly. We use the information you provide to respond and investigate the issue. Please do not post private notes or sensitive information. Privacy questions can be submitted through the same support channel.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date if Noto’s data practices change.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日。

Noto 不向我们发送您的手写内容或语音。App 内没有账号、广告、分析 SDK 或追踪。纸张与辅助线设置保存在本机，设备备份可能包含这些内容。画布垃圾桶清空当前纸张；在总览中选择纸张并点击垃圾桶可删除已保存的纸张。

圈选图片会放入系统剪贴板和临时暂存区。是否粘贴或拖动到其他 App 由您决定，分享后适用接收方的隐私政策。Noto 不把笔记发送给 AI 服务。

只有您点击录音并授权后才使用麦克风。停止、离开选择区域、音频中断、达到两分钟或进入后台时录音结束。音频临时保存在本机，结束后与图片一起复制为视频，由您决定是否分享；我们不接收或转写语音。清空暂存区或关闭 App 会移除会话内容，撤销操作可能在当前会话保留内容。麦克风权限可在系统设置撤销。

免费圈选次数保存在本机。Apple 处理购买并向 App 提供经过验证的购买记录，包括符合条件的家庭共享购买。我们不接收付款信息，不随购买上传手写内容。

本网站托管于 GitHub Pages，没有额外添加分析、广告、Cookie 或第三方媒体嵌入。GitHub 可能按其隐私声明处理访问技术信息。GitHub 问题反馈及用户资料公开可见；我们使用您主动提供的信息回复和排查问题。请勿发布私人笔记或敏感信息。隐私问题可通过上述反馈渠道联系。

数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本页面及生效日期。
