A LITTLE HELP

Back to writing.

The gestures, the small details, and a place to ask for help.

How do I see all my papers?

Pinch inward with two fingers. When the zoom briefly pauses, keep pinching inward to enter the overview. Tap a paper to open it. Settings is in the overview.

How do I add or delete a paper?

Tap the + card in the overview. If an unused blank paper already exists, Noto opens it. To remove papers, touch and hold a paper, select the papers you want to remove, then tap the trash icon. The trash on the writing canvas clears the current paper instead.

How do clippings work?

Choose the lasso and draw around your ink. The selected area becomes an image on the clipboard and in the temporary shelf. Paste or drag it into another app. The shelf is temporary: save anything important elsewhere before clearing it or closing the app.

Can I add a voice note?

After selecting a clipping, tap its microphone and allow recording. Stop recording to copy the image and audio as a video for sharing. Recordings stop automatically after two minutes or when the app goes into the background.

How do I change the paper guides?

Open the template control on the canvas. Choose lines, columns, a grid or dots, and adjust the spacing and visibility. Guides are separate from your handwriting.

What is included for free?

You can keep up to five papers and make 50 clippings. Further clippings or adding a sixth paper requires paid access. Existing papers remain writable. Available plans and current regional prices are shown inside the app.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings from the paper overview, open the purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account used for the purchase. Eligible family purchases are handled through Apple Family Sharing.

How do I manage an annual subscription?

Manage or cancel it in your device’s Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Buying lifetime access does not automatically cancel an existing annual subscription. Purchase terms and prices are shown before you confirm with Apple.

Where are my notes saved?

Papers are stored locally in Noto on this device. Device backups may include them. Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step before saving important work. The clipping shelf is temporary and is not a permanent notebook.

使用帮助

纸张总览：双指向内捏合，在短暂停顿后继续缩小。点击任意纸张即可打开。设置按钮也在总览中。

加纸与删除：点击总览中的＋，已有未使用的空白纸时会直接打开它。长按纸张进入多选，再点垃圾桶删除。画布上的垃圾桶用于清空当前纸张。

圈选与语音：使用套索圈住笔迹，图片会进入剪贴板和暂存区，可以粘贴或拖到其他 App。选择图片后点麦克风可录音，结束后以带声音的视频分享。暂存区不是永久存储，请及时保存重要内容。

免费使用：最多保留 5 张纸、完成 50 次圈选；继续圈选或创建第 6 张纸需要付费，现有纸张仍可书写。恢复购买位于购买页面。购买终身版不会自动取消已有年付订阅。

反馈：通过上面的 GitHub 链接提交问题，请说明设备、系统及 App 版本、复现步骤。反馈内容公开，请勿上传私人笔记或付款信息。
